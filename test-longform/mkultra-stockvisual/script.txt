In nineteen fifty three, the C.I.A. launched a secret program called M.K. Ultra. Its goal: control the human mind. For twenty years, agents dosed unwitting Americans with L.S.D. and tested hypnosis in universities, prisons, and hospitals. Subjects never gave consent. Some were patients. Some were soldiers. Nobody told them they were part of a mind control experiment. In nineteen seventy three, fearing exposure, the director ordered nearly all the records destroyed. What survived was found by accident, in nineteen seventy seven, misfiled in a budget warehouse. Congress held hearings. Survivors came forward. To this day, nobody knows how many people were used, or what is still buried in the shredded pages of the C.I.A.'s darkest experiment.
